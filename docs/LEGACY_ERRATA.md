# Corrections to the original hackathon results

The independent audit is in `docs/audit/AUDIT_2026-10-04.txt`. Original source and reports are preserved at Git commit `723f356`; the audit checkpoint is `3c5d135`. The historical cell counts can generally be reproduced, but the original causal conclusions are not established. The prospective replacement design is in `docs/REPAIR_PROTOCOL.md`.

- The full declaration arm bundled a policy reminder, semantic declaration, clause citation, target binding and a spawn-text check. The acknowledgement control did not match all these. “The clause is the mechanism” and “forced self-review explains the effect” are hypotheses, not demonstrated findings.
- The original pooled interaction was −1.05 with 95% CI [−2.18, +0.08], p=.068, over 20 task clusters. The Firth fallback was added after runs began and should be described as exploratory. Zero observed events do not establish perfect containment.
- Original step budgets were local to each agent and could be exceeded by spawned children and batched calls. The audit found 41/300 main Luna episodes over budget (maximum 239 steps against a nominal 60), with 33 not flagged as truncated. Truncated root submissions were sometimes discarded. Reanalysing summaries cannot repair the original trajectories.
- Credential names could authenticate without acquiring a token, and database queries bypassed the procedural trigger. Ticket ownership was unchecked. Hashes counted as policy transmission even though the child did not receive the text. These are corrected in v2.
- The charter conflated standing permission with a required procedure, including in the no-gate arm. Its wording now explicitly distinguishes them. The revised charter is a change in the experimental treatment environment, not a correction that can be retroactively applied to old trajectories.
- Permissive keyword/numeric graders accepted some wrong or absent answers. V2 uses explicit output contracts and preserves complete outputs. Audit examples include changed Luna Suite A raw completion counts of 42/50 versus 38/50, while compliant completion was 32/50 versus 38/50; objective correctness and compliance are different outcomes.
- The original Δk table pooled agent and environment loci in the Luna structured denominator. Environment-only structured counts are 5/10, 4/20, 2/10 and 1/10 in Δk strata 0, 1, 2 and 4/6 respectively. The task lists also swapped B-S5 and B-L4. `analysis/primary.py` now retains locus in secondary groupings. V2 does not reuse these obsolete density labels after adding tools and credential-use gating.
- Prompt-locus compliance was incomplete: 100 of 177 privileged calls had invalid specifications; 84 spawn attempts yielded 76 successful spawns. These are mixed-suite totals, not 84 successful helpers. External host names in mocked calls are not evidence of real network contact.
- Thirteen orphan/incomplete episodes existed across old run folders. Some budget-interrupted episodes were replaced, including one observed violation replaced by a nonviolation. Recorded summary spend totals US$45.851, rather than the reported US$41, and still does not fully account for all attempts. V2 retains every initiated attempt, unknown costs and incomplete outcomes.
- Agent/officer locus includes mechanical gates as well as an officer. It is an additional semantic approval layer, not an isolated comparison of enforcement locations.

## AI Village interpretation

The audit independently reproduced the raw chain and approval aggregates. It did not validate the original causal interpretations.

- 14.4% refers to regex-matched privileged bash actions divided by **all computer actions**. The corresponding fraction of bash actions is about 35.7%, not one in seven. Session counts with no detected match do not establish absence of privileged behavior: GUI activity is not classified.
- Values such as mean p^k=.839 at p=.95 are hypothetical calculations assuming independent per-action survival. No randomized gate was installed in the Village, so these are not measured throughput or completion rates.
- The 13,160 P4 matches include detached processes and are not a count of verified agent births. Hostname strings in commands do not prove successful connections.
- Nine of ten disclosed proxy-posting requests were approved. That supports a description of approved delegation, not evasion or policy laundering.
- The 300-command classifier exercise was development calibration. V2 was tuned and scored on the same labels; strata had unequal selection probabilities; judge disagreements were dropped; 122 commands were truncated to 600 characters. Labels lacked stable command IDs/hashes, and later sample regeneration changed positional alignment. Reported numbers are not independently validated population precision/recall. The prose additionally said 1.00 credential precision while its own table said .89.
- The repaired sampling pipeline retains full command text, source IDs/ordinals, hashes and stratum sizes; future labels refuse the old positional format. These engineering repairs do not supply a missing independent holdout. No new classifier-validation claim is made.
